00 / Point of view

Engineering is
connection.

A good system is more than its components. It is the quality of the relationships between them — and the clarity with which those relationships are designed, tested and understood.

“I enjoy working at the point where product thinking, engineering depth and real-world constraints meet — turning complex requirements into architectures, interfaces and behaviours a team can build with confidence.”

My work sits across product and systems engineering for EV battery swapping and charging infrastructure. I collaborate with cross-functional teams, reason through edge cases, document system behaviour and use simulation and verification to improve design decisions.
