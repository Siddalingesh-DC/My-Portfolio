01 / THE APPROACH

See the whole.
Solve the detail.

I work where product intent meets system behaviour — connecting people, interfaces, software, electronics and the physical product.

Good systems engineering is not just drawing a diagram. It is asking the right questions, finding the interfaces that matter, anticipating edge cases and making sure the final experience works as one.

At SUN Mobility, my work spans the lifecycle of EV battery-swapping infrastructure: from feature PRDs and system requirements to architecture, charger and cloud integrations, verification support and field-focused solutioning. I enjoy turning ambiguous problems into clear engineering paths with cross-functional teams.
